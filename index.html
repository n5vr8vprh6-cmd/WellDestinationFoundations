The Opportunity

Wellness is becoming the reason people travel.

The booking engines already won the room. Compete on price and availability and you’re competing with software, losing margin every year. But your clients aren’t asking for a cheaper trip; they’re exhausted, and they want to come back different. Wellness is the one category where design, not inventory, still wins, and it’s growing faster than tourism itself. Almost no advisor has been trained for it.

A Category Shift

From wellness hotels to Well Destinations.

For decades, “wellness travel” meant a spa inside a resort. The next evolution is bigger. The destination itself becomes the wellness product: nature, culture, food, movement, recovery, luxury, and community working as one connected system.

A Well Destination isn’t a property you book. It’s a place designed so that wellbeing is the connective tissue of the entire journey. Advisors who understand this stop comparing room rates and start designing outcomes.

  1. Era one

    Sun & Sand Tourism

    Destinations sold beaches and beds. Advisors competed on price and perks.

  2. Era two

    Wellness Amenities

    Hotels added spas, yoga decks, and healthy menus. Wellness lived inside the property line.

  3. Era three · now

    The Well Destination

    An entire destination organized around wellbeing. The advisor becomes the designer of the journey, and the most valuable person in the transaction.

  • Nature

    Rainforest, sea, and volcanic earth as the primary therapy.

  • Culture

    Creole heritage, music, and craft that root the journey in place.

  • Food

    Farm-to-table nourishment as a daily wellness practice.

  • Movement

    Hikes, water, and mindful motion matched to each traveler.

  • Recovery

    Rest, sleep, thermal waters, and genuine restoration.

  • Luxury

    Quiet, considered luxury in service of wellbeing, not instead of it.

  • Community

    Local people and fellow travelers as part of the healing.

  • Longevity

    Journeys designed for long-term health, not just a week away.

Questions

Asked before enrolling.

Do I need a wellness background?

No. Foundations assumes travel-selling experience, not wellness credentials. The program exists precisely because almost no advisor has had formal wellness tourism education. You’ll learn the economics, the framework, and the client language from the ground up.

Is it live or recorded?

Live. Foundations is a three-day virtual intensive with real interaction; that’s why cohorts stay small. Session recordings are made available to enrolled participants for reference after each day.

How much time does it take?

Three consecutive days of live sessions, plus the materials you’ll use afterward. It’s designed for working advisors: intensive by design, so you’re selling with it the following week rather than studying for months.

Does Foundations guarantee a place in the Immersion?

No, and that’s deliberate. Completing Foundations makes you eligible to apply, and graduates receive priority in the application queue. Immersion seats are limited because it’s a working, on-island experience, not a group tour.

I only sell other destinations. Is this still useful?

Yes. Day one and day three (your positioning and the client-conversion system) apply to any destination you sell. Saint Lucia is the flagship case study and your first Well Destination, and the framework is built to extend as more destinations join.

What does enrollment include?

The three-day intensive and the full client-facing system: the WELL Compass discovery script, the client-matching matrix, sellable journey concepts, consultation and fee scripts, your co-branded client guide, and the 90-day activation plan, plus the private community, quarterly updates, and priority Immersion application. No additional purchases inside the program.

When is the next intensive?

We run a three-day LIVE intensive monthly. Reserve on the registration page to see the next available dates and daily session hours in your time zone. Free public intro webinars run monthly alongside it (the next one is on the registration page), and we host customized intro webinars for host agencies and groups on request. The on-island Immersion begins late 2026.

How do I book Saint Lucia journeys and get paid?

Discover Saint Lucia WELL is designed as an add-on: an upsell layered onto a client’s existing itinerary, not a replacement for it. You keep your agency or host relationships and your consortium standing. On customized WELL Destination itineraries you earn 10–15%, with the mechanics running through the Saint Lucia Well Destination collection.

Is there a payment plan?

Standard is $697 USD and VIP is $1,497. Payment plans are available on request.

What’s the difference between Standard and VIP, and can I upgrade later?

Standard ($697) gives you the full program and every deliverable to implement yourself. VIP ($1,497) adds white-glove support that wraps the whole experience: a strategy session and business audit before, VIP access during, and an implementation review, website and messaging critique, 60 days of private support, priority introductions, and a founder roundtable after. Standard is do-it-yourself; VIP is done-with-you, and you can upgrade to VIP anytime.

What if it isn’t for me?

Start with the free webinar; it exists so you can evaluate the thinking before investing. And enrolled advisors are covered: attend the first day of the live intensive, and if it isn’t what you expected, tell us before day two and we’ll refund your tuition in full.
